Others argued it sounded more like the end of the standalone app, not necessarily the end of AI video generation as a capability. In several threads, people tried to separate three different things that are easy to blur together in trend coverage: the Sora brand, the Sora app, and the underlying video-generation technology.

This is why the Reddit lens is useful. It exposes the exact point where public messaging becomes messy.
